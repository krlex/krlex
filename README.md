# Hi! 👋 I'm [Krstan Vještica](https://krletron.cf) 💻

**Senior Linux/FreeBSD System Administrator** based in Novi Sad, Serbia.

- 🐧 11+ years of hands-on experience managing Linux/FreeBSD environments, implementing DevOps practices, and architecting scalable infrastructure.
- 🤝 Co-Founder & Mentor at hackerspace [Tilda Center](https://tilda.center) — sharing knowledge with the local IT community since 2015.
- 🎯 My motivation is to help people do better in DevOps and to show them how to fix and solve SysAdmin problems quickly and efficiently.
- 📫 Reach me at **krle@tilda.center**
